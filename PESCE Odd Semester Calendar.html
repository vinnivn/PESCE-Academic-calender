Things to know about the source calendars

  • The Sem VII calendar lists 24/10/2026 as a Monday, but it is a Saturday. I used the date.
  • The Sem I calendar prints a date as 03/12/202 with the day Wednesday, but 3 Dec 2026 is a Thursday. The Sem III & V calendar has the same events on Wednesday 2 Dec, so I used 2 Dec for Sem I. Confirm with your department.
  • On 04/01/2027 the Sem III & V calendar says "IAT-II marks". I treated it as IAT-III marks, since IAT-II marks are already listed on 02/12/2026.
  • Classes are shown on every Monday to Saturday that has no other event, and Sundays as off. The calendars do not mark public holidays (Dussehra, Diwali, Rajyotsava, Christmas, Sankranti, Republic Day), so check those with your department.
  • Sundays that fall inside an exam window are shown as off. The real exam timetable may differ.
  • Sem VII and Sem III & V are marked "tentative" and Sem I "revised". Dates may still change.
  • The even semester calendars (Sem II, IV, VI, VIII) were not included, so nothing is shown for them. The calendar for Sem VII's even semester from 14 Dec was not provided either.
